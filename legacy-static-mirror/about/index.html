Meet Niki

Experience changes the way you see hair.

Niki Z. has been a hair artisan since 1988, building a career that has taken her from the West Coast to New York City, Europe, and the Middle East.

Her experience spans hair color, extensions, styling, and makeup, but her work has never stopped behind the chair. Niki has trained salon professionals, presented on stages and in salons across the country, and served as an educator and platform artist for leading beauty brands including DevaCurl, Goldwell, Schwarzkopf International, Chromastics, and Microchet.

Today, that depth of experience shapes every client experience at Xtend Yourself.

Niki believes you deserve to understand your hair, your options, and what will actually work for you. Her approach is straightforward and refreshingly honest—tell it like it is, educate first, and create a solution around the person sitting in her chair.

Because the goal has never been to simply give you great hair.

It’s helping you find the look that feels completely like you.
