What happens during a consultation?

Your consultation is all about understanding you, your hair, and your goals. Niki will assess where your hair is now, discuss your options, and recommend a personalized approach. You don’t need to know what service or method you need before reaching out.

back

Will extensions damage my natural hair?

Xtend Yourself is a private, appointment-only salon in Cave Creek, Arizona. Start by submitting an inquiry or requesting a consultation. Nikki will personally coordinate your appointment, and the exact salon location is provided to confirmed clients. Protecting your natural hair is always part of the plan. Niki works with multiple extension and augmentation methods so she can choose an approach based on your hair, density, lifestyle, and desired result rather than using one method for everyone.

back

Do you work with thinning hair or hair loss?

Yes. Hair restoration and augmentation are specialties at Xtend Yourself. Niki works with clients experiencing thinning, fragile hair, loss of density, and hair changes related to illness or chemotherapy.

back

Do I have to know which extension method I want?

Not at all. That’s what your consultation is for. Niki will look at your natural hair, density, lifestyle, and the result you want to achieve, then recommend the extension method that makes the most sense for you.

back

What discounts and payment options do you offer?

Xtend Yourself offers the following discounts:
  • 20% off for military personnel and first responders
  • 5% off for students 
  • 10% off cash payments 

    Credit cards, Venmo, Zelle, and cash are accepted.

back

What is your cancellation policy?

Please provide at least 48 hours’ notice for larger appointments and 24 hours for individual services such as cuts or color. Late cancellations are subject to a 30% cancellation fee.
